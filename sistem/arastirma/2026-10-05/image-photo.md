## Teknikler

### Private, chronologically ordered ingest of iPhone photos and videos
Clone originals read-only (cp -c, then chmod a-w). Read the true capture order with exiftool -T -FileName -DateTimeOriginal -SubSecTimeOriginal -OffsetTimeOriginal -QuickTime:CreationDate -api QuickTimeUTC and sort, which fixes ordering without guessing by season. Convert HEIC to working JPEG, 16-bit PNG or TIFF with sips -m sRGB (or keep Display P3 for Apple-only delivery). Auto-orient. Write a manifest (sha256, timestamp, dimensions). Strip GPS from deliverables with exiftool, or rely on sharp's default stripping.

**Doğrulama:** exiftool -a -G1 -s -gps:all -ee out/* prints nothing. The ICC ProfileDescription is 'sRGB IEC61966-2.1' (or Display P3). File count, dimensions and order match the manifest, and timestamps are monotonic.

### Color-managed P3/HDR to sRGB conversion
Never strip the ICC before converting. Use sips --matchTo the sRGB profile, sharp withIccProfile('srgb'), or pyvips icc_transform('srgb'). Export the SDR rendition explicitly for social. Keep a P3 master if the delivery is Apple-only.

**Doğrulama:** Convert a synthetic Display-P3 test chart. A neutral gray ramp keeps |a*|,|b*| < 1 (colour-science ΔE2000 < 1 versus the expected value). sharp metadata() shows space=srgb with the ICC present. View before and after side by side.

### Build your own LUTs and apply them identically to stills and video
Parametric route: npx hyperframes media-use resolve --type lut --params '{...}' --local-only --project . Pixel-domain route: ffmpeg -f lavfi -i haldclutsrc=8 -frames:v 1 hald8.png, apply the exact grade ops used on a hero still to hald8.png, convert to a 64^3 .cube with colour.LUT3D and write_LUT. Apply everywhere with ffmpeg -vf lut3d=file=look.cube:interp=tetrahedral (use format=rgb48le for 16-bit stills). For iPhone Apple Log, build the Log-to-Rec.709 cube from colour.models.log_decoding_AppleLogProfile plus your tone curve.

**Doğrulama:** cube-validate.mjs returns {ok:true,size}. A neutral ramp through the LUT stays neutral unless warmth is intended. Pixels at 0 or 255 per channel (sharp stats or numpy) are below 0.5%. The LUT is monotonic per channel. hyperframes grade-compare or a contact sheet is viewed before committing.

### Shot-to-shot color matching for montages
Pick a hero frame per scene. Extract a representative frame from each clip (ffmpeg -ss T -frames:v 1). Compute the transfer with color-matcher (mkl or hm-mvgd-hm) or Reinhard mean and std in Lab. Apply that transfer to the identity Hald to bake a per-clip correction cube, then stack it before the global look LUT.

**Doğrulama:** After matching, mean L*a*b* of each clip frame versus the hero has ΔE2000 < 3, and skin regions (Vision face rects) have ΔE < 2 with hue angle within 5 degrees. Clipping does not increase. Review a side-by-side contact sheet.

### Automatic culling (best photo or frame per moment)
Per candidate, compute: Laplacian variance on a 1024 px gray image (blur), median luma and percent clipped (exposure), Vision faceCaptureQuality (0-1), and perceptual-hash duplicates (Hamming distance 6 or less) grouped by timestamp bursts. Rank within each group and keep the top N.

**Doğrulama:** Write a scored JSON and a contact sheet (ffmpeg -vf scale=384:-1,tile=4x3 or sharp composite), and view the sheet. Selected items beat their group median on blur and face quality. Give the user a confirmation list for anything borderline.

### Smart reframing to 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1 (also for Ken Burns paths)
Take the union of Vision face rects and saliency boxes, and fit a crop at the target ratio that keeps faces inside platform safe zones (Stories/Reels: avoid the top ~250 px and bottom ~250-340 px). Fall back to sharp fit:cover with position:'attention'. For push-ins, keep the start and end crops inside the image and place faces on thirds.

**Doğrulama:** Assert every face box is inside the crop with at least 5% margin. Output dimensions are exact (e.g., 1080x1920). View the first, middle and last frames of any Ken Burns path.

### Cutouts with clean edges
Start with the Apple Vision foreground mask. For hair, fur or glass, rerun with rembg birefnet-general (plus -vm ViTMatte for fine detail; note ViTMatte's training-data terms) and decontaminate edge color. Save RGBA PNG and keep the mask separately for later compositing.

**Doğrulama:** Composite over black, white and checker and view at 100%. Alpha coverage % is plausible. For a single subject, connected components at alpha > 0.5 equal 1 (no holes). An edge-halo metric (mean ΔE between a 3 px edge band and the new background) stays below a set threshold.

### Object removal that provably leaves other pixels identical
Build the mask (Vision instance, SAM 2.1 box, or an agent-drawn polygon) and dilate it 8-15 px. Crop context at about 2.5x the bbox, resize to 512, run LaMa ONNX, resize back, and paste only inside the dilated mask with a 4-8 px feather.

**Doğrulama:** PSNR outside the mask is infinite (byte-identical). Boundary gradient magnitude shows no seam. View a 100% crop and a before/after pair.

### Generative edits with identity protection
Use mflux-generate-flux2-edit (klein-4B Q4) for background, sky, lighting or prop changes with a fixed seed, --make-conf and --no-exif. Then re-composite the original pixels for faces and people using feathered Vision person or face masks, so the partner's face is never AI-altered.

**Doğrulama:** Pixel difference inside the protected mask is 0, or ΔE < 1 after the feather. Tesseract OCR (eng+tur, installed) finds no unintended text. The agent views the result. The JSON sidecar records prompt, seed and model revision for reproducibility.

### Upscaling with fidelity checks
Choose the model by damage: realesrgan-x4plus for clean sources, 4xNomos8kSC or 4xLSDIRplusC for JPEG and WhatsApp compression. Upscale 4x, then downsample to the delivery size with lanczos3. Limit faces of real people to about 2x unless reviewed.

**Doğrulama:** Downscale the upscaled image back to the original size and compare with the original: PSNR at least 30 dB and SSIM at least 0.9 (no invented content). Laplacian variance increases at the delivery size. View 100% crops of faces and text.

### Social export presets
Instagram feed: 1080x1350 (4:5, safest), 1080x1080, or 1080x566. The profile grid previews 3:4 (1080x1440 accepted but may crop). Carousels follow the first slide's ratio. Stories/Reels: 1080x1920 with faces and text out of the top ~250 px and bottom ~250-340 px. YouTube thumbnail: 1280x720 at 2 MB or less (desktop allows up to 50 MB). LinkedIn: 1200x627 or 1080x1350. ### Düzeltmeler (seçimlerin önüne geçer)

- **Getting the toolchain working while the Xcode license is unaccepted**: Don't treat Swift, git or /usr/bin/python3 as blocked. Run them with `export DEVELOPER_DIR=/Library/Developer/CommandLineTools`, or call /Library/Developer/CommandLineTools/usr/bin/swift directly; no sudo is needed. Homebrew git 2.51.2 already comes first in PATH. Only `brew` needs the user's sudo fix (`sudo xcodebuild -license accept` or `sudo xcode-select -s /Library/Developer/CommandLineTools`). — gerekçe: Tested 2026-10-05: swiftc typechecked a Vision aesthetics snippet with only the CLT. brew still fails even with DEVELOPER_DIR exported.
- **On-device Apple Vision analysis: subject masks, culling and reframing**: Make a small Swift CLI built with the CLT the main route. It can use foreground instance masks, CalculateImageAestheticsScoresRequest (overallScore, isUtility), VNGenerateImageFeaturePrintRequest for near-duplicate and similar-shot grouping, VNGeneratePersonSegmentationRequest for portrait mattes, saliency and face capture quality. Keep PyObjC as the fallback. — gerekçe: Swift-only APIs are reachable right now, and the native API avoids guessing PyObjC selector names.
- **RAW development with darktable**: Use darktable 5.6.2 (2026-10-04): darktable-5.6.2-arm64.dmg, 88.9 MB, sha256 6ff88e58a2a59cb07b0a1502fea7205e68cd783380a33a3ce7bda68ec29def0c. - **Privacy stripping and verification (photos and videos)**: Verify with `exiftool -a -G1 -s -location:all -ee <files>`; the family-2 Location group covers EXIF GPS plus QuickTime GPSCoordinates in Keys, ItemList and UserData, and XMP. When stripping, keep orientation and color: `exiftool -all= --icc_profile:all -tagsfromfile @ -orientation`, or bake the rotation into the pixels first. For videos, also remux with ffmpeg `-map_metadata -1` and map only the video and audio streams, so iPhone 'mebx' timed-metadata tracks are dropped. — gerekçe: `-gps:all` misses QuickTime location tags, and `-all=` deletes Orientation, which makes images display rotated.

- An Apple Photos library source. osxphotos 0.77.2 (MIT, 2026-09-27) can query people, dates, albums and Photos' own scores, and export originals, edits and Live Photo videos. It needs Full Disk Access, which is the user's decision. ~/Pictures/Photos Library.photoslibrary exists but the folder totals only 1.7 GB, so the originals are probably in iCloud with 'optimize storage', and downloading them involves the user's Apple account.
- Live Photo pairing: match HEIC stills to their MOV clips through ContentIdentifier using exiftool. This gives free 1.5–3 s motion clips for montages without text overlays, which fits the user's feedback.
- License-clean icon and illustration sources for work assets, for example lucide-static (ISC), @tabler/icons (MIT) and Material Symbols (Apache-2.0) from npm; verify each license at install. HyperFrames' icon and image resolve otherwise goes through the HeyGen catalog, which needs an account.
- Apple Log 2 (iPhone 17 Pro, Apple Wide Gamut). colour-science 0.4.7 has the Apple Log transfer functions but no Apple Wide Gamut colorspace. If the user shoots Log 2, the gamut primaries have to come from Apple's documentation. Confidence is low; first ask which iPhone and log mode they use.
